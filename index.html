Set goals by pages, streaks or genres. Up to 15 active challenges free, unlimited with Pro. Plus a customisable daily challenge that doesn't count toward the limit.

Daily challenge

A new goal every day. Complete the challenge before midnight to keep your streak going.

Privacy policy

Your data is yours

Take everything with you whenever you want. Lecturameter exports your library in three ways, all from Settings, under Backups.

CSV: your books in a table

A .csv file with all your books, in a format compatible with Goodreads and StoryGraph. Handy for moving to another app or keeping your list outside your phone.

Full backup (JSON)

Your books, reading sessions, challenges and settings in a single file, made to restore your Lecturameter data inside the app: use it when you switch phones or reinstall. Keep it on your phone or in the cloud: Google Drive on Android, iCloud on iOS.

New in 3.9 / iOS 1.1.0

SQLite: the whole database

A single database file with 23 tables: books, editions, sessions, notes, quotes, reviews, tags, challenges, bingo and yearly summaries, plus ready-made views by day, book and year. Open it with DB Browser for SQLite, Datasette or your own scripts.

It leaves out covers and photos (only the cover link), your settings and your Pro license, and it cannot be restored into the app: to switch phones, use the full backup.

Meet Pagi and pick your style

Pagi is Lecturameter's mascot: an animated bookmark that keeps you company while you read. Pagi adapts to every theme in the app, changing colour to match.
