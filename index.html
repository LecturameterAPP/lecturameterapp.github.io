Three pillars: library, timer, and statistics.

Your books in your private library.

Five shelves to organize your books by status.

  • Search by title, author or ISBN
  • ISBN scanner
  • Full detail: cover, genres, editions
  • Lecturameter remembers the page you left off on so you don't lose your progress
Automatic CSV import. Coming from Goodreads or StoryGraph? Drop your CSV. The app figures out which one it is and does the rest. Any CSV with similar columns (author, publisher, rating, etc.) also imports.

Pick which book you want to read and in which mode: normal or immersive (PRO).

When you end the session, you know how much you've read and at what pace. If the app closes or crashes, the time isn't lost.

  • Normal mode: timer and collapsible minibar
  • Immersive mode: full-screen cover, no distractions
  • Optional ambient sounds (rain, forest, fireplace…)
  • Control from the notification, with the phone locked
  • Both timers are reachable from the counter button at the top

Know yourself as a reader

Challenges, bingos, widgets and more features to get the most out of your reading.

Reading challenges

Set goals by pages, streaks or genres. Up to 15 active challenges free, unlimited with Pro. Plus a customisable daily challenge that doesn't count toward the limit.

Daily challenge

A new goal every day. Complete the challenge before midnight to keep your streak going.

Reading bingo

Two new bingos every month with themed squares. Complete rows, columns or the full card.

Weekly summary

Every Monday, a snapshot of your week: pages read, total time, streak and how it compares to the previous one.

Book widget Free

See the book you're reading right on your home screen: cover, title, author, pages and progress percentage, always up to date. Resizable to fit wherever you want.

Monthly comparison Pro

A quick look at your recent months without opening the app. Bars for each month's pages with the current one highlighted, so you can tell at a glance whether you're doing better or worse this month than last one.
