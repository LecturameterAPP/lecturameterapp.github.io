🍪 Cookie policy

What is stored in your browser when you visit this website, and why.

Updated November 8, 2026

In short
This website uses no analytics or advertising cookies of its own. It stores your preferences (theme, language, dismissed notice) and your choice about this policy in your browser. A third-party widget, StartupBar, only loads if you press “Accept”.

What is stored

Everything lives in your browser's local storage (localStorage or sessionStorage). This website does not write HTTP cookies of its own.

Your preferences (no consent needed)

We store them because you asked for them by using the website. They don't identify anyone and never leave your browser.

  • lm-theme · localStorage · remembers whether you picked the light or dark theme · until you clear it.
  • lm_lang · localStorage · remembers the language you picked in the selector · until you clear it.
  • promo-dismissed · localStorage · remembers that you closed the top bar · until you clear it.
  • lm_consent · localStorage · stores your choice in the cookie notice, with its version and date · until you change or clear it.

Only if you accept

If you press “Accept”, these third-party items load. If you press “Reject”, none of this loads and the website works the same.

  • StartupBar (startupbar.co) · a widget with a third-party iframe, which stores startupbar:sid in sessionStorage (until you close the tab) and sends an impressions signal. Every page of the website loads it.
  • EmailJS (cdn.jsdelivr.net) · a library on the contact page, served by jsDelivr.

What we don't do

We use no analytics of our own, no advertising, no profiling and no tracking cookies. We don't sell data either. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log your IP when serving the pages under its own policy.

Change your choice

You can change it any time with “Cookie preferences” in the footer of any page. You can also clear this website's data from your browser settings. If you withdraw your consent, the page reloads without the third-party items.
