Idatha yakho yokufunda ingeyakho
I-Lecturameter 1999 ayinaseva yayo ngokwayo yelabhulali yakho. Izincwadi zakho, iziqeshana nezilungiselelo zihlala kudivayisi yakho kuphela nasemakhophini akho esipele. Azikho iziakhawunti, akukho nkampani yamafu eyobunikazi, azikho izikhangiso, futhi asisoze sathengisa idatha yakho.

Inqubomgomo ephelele

Ibuyekezwe kokugcina: Okthoba 20, 2026

Leli khasi lichaza, ngolimi olulula, ukuthi yiyiphi idatha ye-Lecturameter 1999 egcinayo nokuthi yini ephuma kudivayisi yakho.

1. Lapho idatha yakho ihlala khona

Izincwadi zakho, iziqeshana zokufunda nezilungiselelo kugcinwa kudivayisi yakho kuphela. Amakhophi esipele namafayela e-CSV alondolozwa njengefayela noma ikuphi ukhetha kona usebenzisa isikhethi samafayela sesistimu. Azikho iziakhawunti zabasebenzisi futhi akukho ifu le-Lecturameter 1999.

2. Azikho izibalo zomuntu wesithathu noma izikhangiso

  • Asisebenzisi ama-SDK ezibalo zabantu besithathu futhi asisebenzisi amathuluzi okukhangisa noma okulandelela.
  • Asibonisi izikhangiso.
  • Asisoze sathengisa idatha yakho noma sayisebenzisela ukukhangisa noma ukwenza iphrofayili.

3. Izibalo zokusetshenziswa ezingaziwa negama nezinkombandlela zokuphahlazeka (ezingakhethwa)

Izibalo zokusetshenziswa ezingaziwa negama (ezingakhethwa, zivuliwe ngokwemvelo): uhlelo lokusebenza luthumela kuseva ye-Lecturameter uqobo (ebikwa ku-Cloudflare) isihlonzi sokufaka esingahleliwe esenziwe uhlelo lokusebenza ngokwalo, esingaboshelwe kuwe, ku-ID yesikhangiso sedivayisi noma kunoma iyiphi iakhawunti, kanye nenguqulo yohlelo lokusebenza, uhlelo lokusebenza namagama emicimbi yomkhiqizo (isibonelo: incwadi yokuqala yengeziwe, iseshini yokuqala, ukungenisa kuphelile), kanye nezifanekiso zokubuya zosuku lwe-1, lwe-7 nolwe-30. Iqoqo elilodwa ngosuku ubuningi. Akukho okuqukethwe kwelabhulali futhi akukho datha yomuntu othunyelwayo.

Izinkombandlela zokuphahlazeka (ezingakhethwa, zivuliwe ngokwemvelo): uma uhlelo lokusebenza luphahlazeka noma lubanda, luthumela kuseva ye-Lecturameter uqobo inguqulo yohlelo lokusebenza, inguqulo ye-OS, umkhiqizi nemodeli yedivayisi, ulimi nemininingwane yobuchwepheshe yephutha, ukuze kulungiswe iphutha kuphela.

Zombili izinketho zingavalwa ku-Usizo > Ubumfihlo ngaphakathi kohlelo lokusebenza.

4. Usesho lwezincwadi

Umbhalo owuthayiphayo uma useshela incwadi noma i-ISBN uthunyelwa ku-API yekhathalogu ye-Lecturameter futhi, ngayo, ku-Google Books, ukuletha isihloko, umbhali, amakhasi nekhava. Akukho datha yakho yomuntu ethunyelwayo, kuphela igama losesho noma i-ISBN. Amakhava alandwa kukheli lesithombe lencwadi ngayinye.

5. Ukuthenga ngaphakathi kohlelo lokusebenza namathiphu

I-Lecturameter 1999 inokuthenga kanye okungakhethwa (okungasetshenziswa) okuvula izengezo zokuhlobisa: iphakheji yezindikimba (nezingemuva zayo, imisindo nezivimbeli zesikrini), izivimbeli zesikrini, izingubo zikaPagi nenhlanganisela yakho konke. Inamathiphu akhethwayo (ukuthenga okusetshenziswayo) angavuli lutho. Aphethwe yi-Google Play (Android) noma i-App Store (iOS). Uhlelo lokusebenza lunikezwa kuphela isiqinisekiso sokuthenga ukuze kusebenze lokho obukuthengile futhi alukaze lubone noma lugcine imininingwane yakho yokukhokha.

6. Izimvume zohlelo lokusebenza

  • I-inthanethi: ukuseshela izincwadi, izibalo ezingaziwa negama nezinkombandlela zokuphahlazeka (uma kuvuliwe), nokuthenga.
  • Isimo senethiwekhi (Android): ukwazi ukuthi ingabe kukhona ukuxhumeka ngaphambi kokuseshela noma ukuthumela idatha.
  • Ukukhokha kwe-Google Play (Android): ngokuthenga namathiphu.
  • Aluceli izaziso, ikhamera, indawo noma oxhumana nabo. Amakhophi esipele, amafayela e-CSV namakhava egalari akhethwa ngesikhethi sesistimu, esingadingi mvume.

7. Thintana nathi

Uma unemibuzo ngobumfihlo, bhalela lecturameter.app@gmail.com.

8. Izinguquko kule nqubomgomo

Uma le nqubomgomo ishintsha, sizobuyekeza leli khasi elifanayo ngosuku olusha ngaphezulu.
