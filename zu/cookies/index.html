🍪 Inqubomgomo yamakhukhi

Okugcinwa kusiphequluli sakho uma uvakashela le webhusayithi, nokuthi kungani.

Kubuyekezwe ngo-Novemba 8, 2026

Ngamafuphi
Le webhusayithi ayisebenzisi amakhukhi ayo okuhlaziya noma ezikhangiso. Igcina kusiphequluli sakho okuthandayo (itimu, ulimi, isaziso esivaliwe) nokukhetha kwakho ngale nqubomgomo. Iwijethi yenkampani yesithathu, i-StartupBar, ilayisha kuphela uma ucindezela “Yamukela”.

Okugcinwayo

Konke kusesitorejini sasendaweni sesiphequluli sakho (localStorage noma sessionStorage). Le webhusayithi ayibhali amakhukhi ayo e-HTTP.

Okuthandayo kwakho (akudingi imvume)

Siyakugcina ngoba ukucele ngokusebenzisa iwebhusayithi. Akuhlonzi muntu futhi akuphumi esiphequluli sakho.

  • lm-theme · localStorage · ikhumbula ukuthi ukhethe itimu ekhanyayo noma emnyama · kuze kube uyasula.
  • lm_lang · localStorage · ikhumbula ulimi olukhethe kusikhethi · kuze kube uyasula.
  • promo-dismissed · localStorage · ikhumbula ukuthi uvale ibha ephezulu · kuze kube uyasula.
  • lm_consent · localStorage · igcina ukukhetha kwakho esazisweni samakhukhi, nenguqulo nedethi yaso · kuze kube uyashintsha noma uyasula.

Kuphela uma wamukela

Uma ucindezela “Yamukela”, lezi zinto zenkampani yesithathu ziyalayisha. Uma ucindezela “Yenqaba”, akukho kulokhu okulayishwayo futhi iwebhusayithi isebenza ngendlela efanayo.

  • StartupBar (startupbar.co) · iwijethi ene-iframe yenkampani yesithathu, egcina startupbar:sid ku-sessionStorage (kuze kube uvala ithebhu) futhi ithumela uphawu lokubukwa. Ikhasi ngalinye lewebhusayithi liyayilayisha.
  • EmailJS (cdn.jsdelivr.net) · umtapo ekhasini lokuxhumana, otholakala ku-jsDelivr.

Esingakwenzi

Asisebenzisi ukuhlaziya kwabantu besithathu, izikhangiso, ukwenza amaphrofayela noma amakhukhi okulandela. Okuwukuphela kwento esiyisebenzisayo isibali sethu sezivakashi, singenamakhukhi futhi singenabantu besithathu (imininingwane enqubomgomweni yobumfihlo). Asithengisi nedatha. Iwebhusayithi ibanjwe ku-GitHub Pages, engabhala i-IP yakho uma ihambisa amakhasi ngokwenqubomgomo yayo.

Shintsha ukukhetha kwakho

Ungakushintsha noma nini nge-“Okukhethwayo kwamakhukhi” ngaphansi kwanoma yikuphi ikhasi. Ungasula futhi idatha yale webhusayithi kuzilungiselelo zesiphequluli. Uma ubuyisa imvume, ikhasi lilayisha kabusha ngaphandle kwezinto zenkampani yesithathu.
