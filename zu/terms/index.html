📄 Imigomo Yesevisi

Imigomo ecacile, akukho okuncane okubhalwe kancane.

NguVíctor (Lecturameter) · Ibuyekezwe ngo-Septhemba 14, 2026

Ngokulanda, ukufaka, noma ukusebenzisa i-Lecturameter ("Uhlelo lokusebenza"), uyavuma kule Migomo Yesevisi. Uma ungavumi, ungasebenzisi Uhlelo lokusebenza.

1. Ubani othuthukisa Uhlelo lokusebenza

I-Lecturameter ithuthukiswa nguVíctor Parra Rueda, ohlala eSpain. Xhumana: lecturameter.app@gmail.com

Uhlelo lokusebenza lusatshalaliswa ngokukhethekile nge-Google Play (Android) ne-App Store (iOS). Kuzo zombili izinkundla, i-Google ne-Apple zisebenza njengabathengisi abarekhodiwe: baphatha izinkokhelo, izintela nemirasidi.

2. Uhlelo lokusebenza nedatha yakho

I-Lecturameter ayidingi iakhawunti yomsebenzisi. Yonke idatha yakho (ilabhulali, iziqeshana, izibalo) igcinwa endaweni kudivayisi yakho. Nguwe kuphela ophethe ngokugcina amakhophi esipele edatha yakho. Uhlelo lokusebenza lunezici zokuthumela ngaphandle nokungenisa zalokhu.

3. Inguqulo yamahhala nenguqulo ye-Pro

Inguqulo yamahhala — ukufinyelela ezicini eziyinhloko zokulandelela ukufunda ngaphandle kwezindleko.

I-Pro ku-Android — ukuthenga kanye

Ithengwa ngenkokhelo eyodwa nge-Google Play. Uma isithengiwe, ukufinyelela kuhlala njalo futhi akudingi ukuvuselelwa. Amanani afaka izintela ezifanele.

I-Pro ku-iOS — ukuthenga kanye

  • Inkokhelo eyodwa: ithengwa njengokuthenga okungasetshenziswa ngaphakathi kohlelo lokusebenza nge-App Store. Uma isithengiwe, ukufinyelela kuhlala njalo. Akuyona imibhalisile futhi ayivuseleleki.
  • Ukuzama mahhala: izinsuku eziyi-7, ngaphandle kwekhadi noma imininingwane yokukhokha. Akukho okukhokhiswayo uma kuphela: Uhlelo lokusebenza lubuyela enguqulweni yamahhala ngaphandle kwalokho uthenge i-Pro.
  • Buyisela ukuthenga: kuyatholakala ngaphakathi kohlelo lokusebenza uma ushintsha amadivayisi noma ufaka kabusha.
  • I-Pro phakathi kwezinkundla: uma usuyithengile i-Pro ku-Android noma i-iOS, ungayivula kwamanye amadivayisi akho ngekhodi eyenziwe ngaphakathi kohlelo lokusebenza, ngaphandle kokukhokha futhi.

4. Ilungelo lokuhoxa (abasebenzisi be-EU)

Ngaphansi kweDirective 2011/83/EU, abathengi be-EU banelungelo lokuhoxa esivumelwaneni phakathi nezinsuku zekhalenda ezingu-14 kusukela ekuphetheni kwaso.

Nokho, ngokuqeda ukuthenga noma ukuqala ukufinyelela okuqukethwe kwe-Pro, uvuma ngokusobala ukuqala ngokushesha kokuhlinzekwa kwesevisi futhi uyavuma ukuthi ulahlekelwa ilungelo lokuhoxa, ngokuhambisana ne-Article 16(m) ye-Directive.

Ukusebenzisa ilungelo lokuhoxa lapho kufanele, xhumana ne-lecturameter.app@gmail.com phakathi nesikhathi esishiwo.

5. Ukubuyiselwa kwemali

I-Android: kuncike enqubomgomo yokubuyiselwa kwemali ye-Google Play. Ungacela ukubuyiselwa kwemali phakathi namahora angu-48 ngemuva kokuthenga nge-Google Play.

I-iOS: kuncike enqubomgomo yokubuyiselwa kwemali ye-Apple. Cela ukubuyiselwa kwemali ku-reportaproblem.apple.com. Umthuthukisi akakwazi ukucubungula ukubuyiselwa kwemali ngokuqondile.

Okungenhla akuthinti amalungelo abathengi angakwazi ukuyekwa ngaphansi komthetho we-EU.

6. Amakhodi okuthengisa

Umthuthukisi angase anikeze amakhodi anikeza ukufinyelela kwe-Pro mahhala noma ngesaphulelo, kuncike ezimeni ezithile (inkundla, ukusebenza, ukusetshenziswa kanye). Amakhodi awashintshwa ngemali futhi awadlulisekile.

7. Ifa lengqondo

Uhlelo lokusebenza, umklamo walo, ikhodi nezithonjana kungezomthuthukisi. Ugcina wonke amalungelo okuqukethwe okufaka kuhlelo lokusebenza (izincwadi, amanothi, iziqeshana), okuhlala kudivayisi yakho.

8. Ubumfihlo

Ukuphathwa kwedatha kuchazwe ku-Nqubomgomo Yobumfihlo, eyingxenye yale Migomo.

9. Ukusetshenziswa okwamukelekile

Uyavuma ukungenzi i-reverse engineering ye-Uhlelo lokusebenza, ukungagwemi izindlela zokuqinisekisa ze-Pro, noma ukungasebenzisi Uhlelo lokusebenza ngezinhloso eziphambene nomthetho.

10. Ukukhawulelwa kwesibopho

Uhlelo lokusebenza lunikezwa "njengoba lunjalo". Ngezinga elivunyelwe umthetho, isibopho esiphezulu somthuthukisi asisoze saphuma esamba owasikhokha ezinyangeni eziyi-12 ngaphambi kwesimangalo. Lokhu kukhawulelwa akusebenzi ekulimaleni okubangelwa ukwenza kabi ngamabomu noma ukunganaki okukhulu komthuthukisi, nakumalungelo abathengi angakwazi ukuyekwa ngaphansi komthetho we-EU.

Umthuthukisi akenzi isibopho ngokulahlekelwa idatha okubangelwa ukwehluleka kwedivayisi noma ukukhipha Uhlelo lokusebenza. Sebenzisa izici zokuthumela ngaphandle ukugcina amakhophi esipele.

11. Izinguquko kule Migomo

Umthuthukisi angase abuyekeze le Migomo. Izinguquko ezibalulekile zizoxoxwa okungenani izinsuku ezingu-30 kusengaphambili ngesaziso Ohlelweni lokusebenza noma kule webhusayithi. Ukuqhubeka nokusebenzisa kuyakwamukela.

12. Umthetho olawulayo nesifunda sokuthonya

Le Migomo ilawulwa ngumthetho waseSpain. Kusebenza imithetho yokuvikelwa kwabathengi be-EU, enikeza abathengi ilungelo lokufaka izimangalo ezinkantolo zendawo abahlala kuyo. Akukho ukulamula okuphoqelelwe kubathengi be-EU (Directive 93/13/EEC).

13. Ukuxazululwa kwezingxabano ku-inthanethi (EU)

Abathengi be-EU bangasebenzisa inkundla yokuxazulula izingxabano ku-inthanethi ye-European Commission: ec.europa.eu/consumers/odr.
