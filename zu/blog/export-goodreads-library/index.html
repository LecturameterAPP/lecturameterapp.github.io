Indlela yokungenisa ilabhulali yakho ye-Goodreads (ne-StoryGraph, Bookmory, Fable…) ku-Lecturameter

I-Lecturameter ifunda amafayela ethunyelwe ngaphandle avela ku-Goodreads, StoryGraph, Bookmory, Fable/ShelfBridge nakwezinye izinhlelo zokufunda ezimbalwa. Lokhu okuthunyelwe kudlula kwesinye nesinye.

Ilabhulali yakho ihlalaphi

Uma usuvele uqopha ukufunda kwakho kwenye indawo futhi ufuna ukukuthutha, i-Lecturameter yamukela amafayela athunyelwe ngaphandle avela ezinhlelweni eziningi ezidumile zokuqopha: Goodreads, StoryGraph, Bookmory, Fable (ngesandiso se-ShelfBridge) nezimbalwa ezincane. Lokhu okuthunyelwe kuhlanganisa isinyathelo sokuthumela ngaphandle somthombo ngamunye, ukuze ukhethe osebenzisayo ulandele izinyathelo.

Akukho lutho lapha oludinga i-akhawunti ye-Lecturameter. Ifayela lihlala efonini yakho.

Umlingisi wokungenisa

Uma uvula i-Lecturameter okokuqala, i-Pagi ikubuza ukuthi ufuna ukwenzani. Esinye sezinketho ukuthi “Ngivela kwenye i-app”. Khetha lokho bese ufika kumlingisi wokungenisa.

Umlingisi uwuhlu lwemithombo. Uthinta osebenzisayo, uwunike ifayela, futhi i-Lecturameter ifunda uhlaka. Umlingisi ofanayo ukhona nakwa Izilungiselelo, Ngenisa, ukuze ungawusebenzisa kamuva.

Goodreads (CSV)

I-Goodreads isakuvumela ukuthumela ilabhulali yakho ngaphandle, kodwa inketho ifihliwe futhi isebenza kuphela kusayithi ledeskithophu. Kocingo ayikho.

  1. Vula goodreads.com bese ungena.
  2. Chofoza “My Books” ekuzulweni okuphezulu.
  3. Ebhanini eliseceleni kwesobunxele, ngaphansi kwe-“Tools”, chofoza “Import and export”.
  4. Funa isigaba esithi “Export your books” bese uchofoza “Export Library”.
  5. Linda umzuzu. Uma ifayela selilungile ubona isixhumanisi esifana ne-“Your export from Goodreads” esilanda i-CSV.

Ifayela linomugqa owodwa encwadini ngayinye nemigqa engaba amashumi amathathu. Okubalulekile: isihloko, umbhali, i-ISBN, isilinganiso sakho, izinsuku zokufunda, izinsuku zokwengeza, amashelufu (akhethekile nawokukhetha) nombhalo wokubuyekeza kwakho. Amanothi acashile ahamba kukholomu ethi “Private Notes”, okulula ukuyeqa.

Isilinganiso sisebenzisa izinkanyezi ezigcwele, hhayi izinkanyezi ezingxenye. Izinsuku zisefomethi “YYYY/MM/DD”. Abangani, amakhomenti, ama-like namaqembu kuhlala ku-Goodreads. Lokho akuyona imikhawulo ye-Lecturameter, anakho nje ukuba sefayelini.

StoryGraph (CSV)

I-StoryGraph nayo ithumela ngaphandle kuwebhu. Iya ku-app.thestorygraph.com, vula Settings, bese Manage Account, usebenzise inketho ye-Export. Uthola i-CSV enezincwadi zakho, amashelufu, izilinganiso, umbhalo wokubuyekeza, izinsuku namathegi. I-Lecturameter iyifunda ngqo futhi imephe izinsimu.

Bookmory (XLSX)

Ngaphakathi kwe-Bookmory vula Settings, bese Backup, bese “Export to Excel”. Uthola i-xlsx enamashidi ahlukene ezincwadi, izilinganiso namaseshini okufunda. I-Lecturameter ifunda i-xlsx ngqo; awudingi ukuyiguqula.

Fable / ShelfBridge (CSV ngesandiso)

I-Fable uqobo ayinayo inkinobho yokuthumela ngaphandle. Iphrojekthi yomphakathi i-ShelfBridge yisandiso sesiphequluli esifunda idatha yakho ye-Fable bese sikhiqiza i-CSV ehambisana ne-Fable. I-Lecturameter yamukela leli fayela endaweni ethi Fable / ShelfBridge yomlingisi.

Book Tracker (CSV)

Vula i-Book Tracker, iya ku-Settings > Export bese ukhetha i-CSV. Uthola ifayela elinezincwadi zakho, izilinganiso, izinsuku namashelufu. Lifake endaweni ye-Book Tracker yomlingisi bese i-Lecturameter iyalifunda.

Ezinye izinhlelo

Umlingisi unendawo ethi “Ezinye izinhlelo” ye-Babelio, Pagebound, Bookshelf, Delibrista nezifanayo. Uma uhlelo osisebenzisayo lungakhiqiza i-CSV noma i-XLSX, i-Lecturameter izama ukuthola uhlaka ngokuzenzekelayo.

Uma eyakho ingaziwa, indlela yokubuyela ilula: vula ifayela kusipredishithi, gcina amakholomu esihloko, umbhali, isilinganiso, izinsuku neshelufu, ulilondoloze njenge-CSV, bese ulingenisa njenge-Goodreads. Umfundi we-Goodreads yiyona evumela kakhulu kunazo zonke.

Okuphatheka nokungaphatheki

Kuyo yonke imithombo, izinto ezifanayo ziyahamba: izincwadi, amashelufu, izilinganiso, izinsuku namanothi omuntu siqu noma ukubuyekeza.

Izinto ezifanayo zisala emuva kusevisi ngayinye futhi: abangani, amakhomenti, ama-like, amaqembu nokulandelwa. Lokho kuyimikhawulo yamafayela athunyelwe ngaphandle uqobo, hhayi i-Lecturameter. Akukho lutho lokuthutha ngoba azange abe sefayelini.

Ngemuva kokungenisa

Uma weqe umlingisi ekuqaleni kokuqala, ukungenisa kukhona nakwa Izilungiselelo, Ngenisa. Kufunda amashelufu efayelini lakho bese kubuza ukuthi yimaphi ofuna ukuwathutha. Izilinganiso, izinsuku namanothi kuza nezincwadi. Akukho lutho oluya kuseva. Ilabhulali yakho iphelela efonini yakho.
