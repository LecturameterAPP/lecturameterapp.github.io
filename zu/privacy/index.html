Idatha yakho yokufunda ingeyakho
I-Lecturameter ayinaseva yayo ngokwayo yelabhulali yakho. Izincwadi zakho, iziqeshana nezibalo zihlala efonini yakho kuphela nasemakhophini akho esipele. Azikho izikhangiso, akukho ukulandelela, azikho iziakhawunti zabasebenzisi, futhi asisoze sathengisa idatha yakho noma sayisebenzisela ukukhangisa.

Inqubomgomo ephelele

Ibuyekezwe kokugcina: Okthoba 30, 2026

Leli khasi lifana nenqubomgomo yobumfihlo oyibona ngaphakathi kohlelo lokusebenza. Lichaza, ngolimi olulula, ukuthi yiyiphi idatha ye-Lecturameter egcinayo nokuthi yini ephuma kudivayisi yakho.

1. Lapho idatha yakho ihlala khona

Izincwadi zakho, iziqeshana zokufunda, izibalo, izinselelo ne-bingo kugcinwa kudivayisi yakho kuphela (uhlelo lokusebenza lusebenza kuqala ngaphandle kwenethiwekhi). Uma uvula amakhophi esipele, agcinwa noma ikuphi ukhetha kona:

  • I-Android: ifayela lasendaweni kudivayisi yakho, noma i-Google Drive yakho. Ku-Drive, uhlelo lokusebenza lungabona futhi luthinte kuphela amafayela olidale lona lapho (ububanzi be-drive.file), alukaze lubone okunye kwe-Drive yakho.
  • I-iOS: ifayela lasendaweni kufolda yohlelo lokusebenza (elifinyeleleka kusuka kuhlelo lwe-Files), noma i-iCloud yakho. Ikhophi yesipele ye-iCloud isebenzisa kuphela isikhwama sohlelo lokusebenza ku-iCloud yakho; i-Apple iphatha ukubethelwa nokuvumelanisa. Ungenza futhi ikhophi yesipele ku-Google Drive yakho: uhlelo lokusebenza lubona kuphela amafayela olidale lona lapho, akukho okunye kweakhawunti yakho.

Akekho ngaphandle kwakho ongabona lawo mafayela.

2. Akukho zibalo zomuntu wesithathu, azikho izikhangiso, azikho iziakhawunti

  • Asisebenzisi ama-SDK ezibalo zabantu besithathu futhi asisebenzisi amathuluzi okukhangisa noma okulandelela phakathi kwezinhlelo zokusebenza.
  • Asibonisi izikhangiso.
  • Awudingi ukudala noma iyiphi iakhawunti ukuze usebenzise uhlelo lokusebenza.
  • Asisoze sathengisa idatha yakho noma sayisebenzisela ukukhangisa noma ukwenza iphrofayili.
  • Siqoqa izibalo zethu zokusetshenziswa ezingakhethwa ezingaziwa negama kanye nezinkombandlela zokuphahlazeka, zombili zichazwe esigabeni 3. Ungazivala ku-Izilungiselelo > Usizo nokuthi mayelana > Ubumfihlo.

3. Izibalo zokusetshenziswa ezingaziwa negama nezinkombandlela zokuphahlazeka

Izibalo zokusetshenziswa ezingaziwa negama (ezingakhethwa): uhlelo lokusebenza luthumela kuseva ye-Lecturameter uqobo (ebikwa ku-Cloudflare) isihlonzi sokufaka esingahleliwe esenziwe uhlelo lokusebenza ngokwalo, esishintsha njalo ezinsukwini ezingu-90 futhi asixhunyiwe kubuwena, ku-ID yesikhangiso sedivayisi noma kunoma iyiphi iakhawunti; inguqulo yohlelo lokusebenza, uhlelo lokusebenza namagama emicimbi yomkhiqizo (isibonelo: incwadi yokuqala yengeziwe, ukuzama kuqalile noma kuphelile, ikhadi lokwabelana lisetshenzisiwe), kanye nokuthi ubuya yini kuhlelo lokusebenza ngosuku lwe-1, lwe-7 nolwe-30. Akukho lutho oluvela elabhulali yakho, azikho izihloko zezincwadi futhi akukho idatha yomuntu othumelwayo. Le datha ayisetshenziswa ekukhangiseni, ekulandeleleni phakathi kwezinhlelo zokusebenza, noma ekwenzeni iphrofayili, futhi ayithengiswa kubantu besithathu.

Izinkombandlela zokuphahlazeka (ezingakhethwa): uma uhlelo lokusebenza luvaleka ngokungalindelekile noma, ku-Android, lushiya ukuphendula, luthumela umbiko wobuchwepheshe kuseva ye-Lecturameter: uhlobo nomlayezo wephutha, umkhondo wekhodi yohlelo lokusebenza (ekumiseni kwe-Android, ukhoko oluyinhloko kuphela), inguqulo yohlelo lokusebenza, imodeli nomkhiqizi wedivayisi, inguqulo yesistimu nolimi. Ayifaki ilabhulali yakho, amanothi akho noma noma yisiphi isihlonzi. Umlayezo wephutha wobuchwepheshe, kodwa kwezinye izikhathi ezingavamile ungafaka ucezu lwedatha eyidale. Ungakuvala ku-Izilungiselelo > Usizo nokuthi mayelana > Ubumfihlo > Izinkombandlela zokuphahlazeka.

Zombili izinketho zingavalwa ku-Izilungiselelo > Usizo nokuthi mayelana > Ubumfihlo (izishintshi “Izibalo zokusetshenziswa” kanye “Izinkombandlela zokuphahlazeka”).

4. Ukuseshela nokuskena izincwadi

Uma useshela incwadi noma uskena i-ISBN, lowo mbhalo noma ikhodi iya kuseva yekhathalogu ye-Lecturameter (ebikwa ku-Cloudflare), ebuza amakhathalogu ezincwadi omphakathi ngawe (Google Books, Open Library, Wikidata, AniList, MangaUpdates, Kitsu) ukuletha isihloko, ikhava noma umbhali. Iseva yethu ayigcini ikheli lakho le-IP, futhi imiphumela igcinwa ekhasheni lesikhashana (kusuka emahoreni ambalwa kuya ezinsukwini ezingu-30) elingaxhunyiwe kunoma ubani. Akukho datha yakho yomuntu othunyelwa kulezi zicelo.

I-Lecturameter ifaka futhi ikhathalogu yezincwadi yasendaweni kudivayisi yakho: isizindalwazi sayo esinemisebenzi eyizi-12,000 elandwa kanye nohlelo lokusebenza. Yiyo evumela ukuseshela izincwadi ngaphandle kwenethiwekhi. Leyo khathalogu iyinto yohlelo lokusebenza, ayinayo idatha yakho, futhi akukho ongasesha kuyo noma okutholayo okusoze kwashiya idivayisi yakho.

Uma ikhathalogu ye-Lecturameter ingatholi lutho ngosesho lwakho, noma wengeza incwadi eyayingekho kuyo, iseva yethu igcina lokho okuseshile (umbhalo, kuze kufike ezinhlamvwini ezingu-200, noma i-ISBN), uhlobo losesho, izihlonzi zencwadi oyengezayo, usuku nokuhashwa kwe-user agent yohlelo lokusebenza, ukuze sazi ukuthi yiyiphi imisebenzi engekho futhi sikwazi ukuyengeza. Ayigcini ikheli lakho le-IP noma isihlonzi sakho noma sedivayisi yakho, futhi igcina lokhu izinsuku ezingu-90 ubuningi. Lokhu kusiza bonke abasebenzisi ukuthuthukisa ikhathalogu, ngakho ayinaso isishintshi ku-Izilungiselelo. Ungafunda ukuthi leyo khathalogu iyaphi kuphi ku-lesi sigaba setshalo.

5. Impendulo nezinhlolovo

Ukuthumela impendulo noma ukuphendula inhlolovo ngaphakathi kohlelo lokusebenza kuyikho konke okuzikhethelayo. Uma ukhetha ukwenza kanjalo, umbhalo owubhalile uthunyelwa kanye nolwazi lwezobuchwepheshe ledivayisi (imodeli, inguqulo ye-OS (Android noma iOS), nenguqulo yohlelo lokusebenza) kumthuthukisi we-Lecturameter, ukuze akwazi ukuyifunda nokuthuthukisa uhlelo lokusebenza. Uma ukhetha futhi ukunamathisela amalogi ohlelo lokusebenza noma izithombe zesikrini, nalokho kuyathunyelwa; kokubili kuyakhethwa futhi kufakwa kuphela uma ukwengeza.

6. I-Lecturameter Pro nokuthola ngamakhodi

Inkokhelo ye-Lecturameter Pro iphethwe yi-Google Play (ku-Android) noma i-Apple App Store (ku-iOS): uhlelo lokusebenza alukaze lubone noma lugcine imininingwane yakho yokukhokha. Uma uthenga noma ubuyisela ukuthenga, isihlonzi sokuthenga sesitolo sithunyelwa kuseva yelayisense ye-Lecturameter ukuze kuqinisekiswe ukuthenga kuphela. Uma uthola ikhodi yokukhushulwa, leyo khodi nesihlonzi sedivayisi kuthunyelwa kuseva yokuthola ye-Lecturameter, ukuze kuqinisekiswe ikhodi nokukhawulela ukuthi mangaki amadivayisi angayisebenzisa.

7. Isihlonzi sedivayisi

Ukuvimbela ukusetshenziswa kabi kokuzama mahhala nokuqinisekisa ilayisense yakho ye-Pro, uhlelo lokusebenza luthumela isihlonzi sedivayisi kuseva yelayisense ye-Lecturameter (ebikwa ku-Cloudflare): i-Android ID ku-Android, isihlonzi somthengisi ku-iOS. Sithunyelwa uma uqala ukuzama mahhala, uma uthola noma ubuyisela i-Pro ngekhodi, uma ubuyisela ukuthenga, nalapho uhlelo lokusebenza luzama kabusha ukubhalisa ukuzama okulindile lapho luqala. Asisetshenziswa ekukhangiseni noma ekwenzeni iphrofayili futhi asixhunyiwe egameni lakho.

8. Ukuvumelanisa nesifundi se-e (KOSync)

Kuyakhethwa: uma ungaxhumi isifundi se-e, akukho lutho olusithunyelwa. Uma uxhuma esisodwa (KOReader, CrossPoint noma esinye esihambisana ne-KOSync), uhlelo lokusebenza luthumela kuseva yokuvumelanisa ye-Lecturameter (ebikwa ku-Cloudflare, eNtshonalanga Yurophu) igama lomsebenzisi lokuvumelanisa (izinhlamvu ezingu-3 kuya kwezingu-32, ngaphandle kwe-imeyili noma idatha yomuntu; akuyona iakhawunti ye-Lecturameter) nokhiye osetshenziswa isifundi sakho se-e, iseva ewugcina njengehashi enosawoti kuphela. Ngencwadi ngayinye evumelanisiwe igcina isihlonzi sedokhumenti, indawo, iphesenti, igama nesihlonzi sedivayisi, usuku futhi, uma isifundi sakho se-e sizithumela, isihloko, ababhali negama lefayela. Asigcini ikheli lakho le-IP: imikhawulo yezicelo ibalwa ngokushesha yi-Cloudflare.

Ungayisusa kanjani: uma unqamula isifundi se-e, khetha "Futhi susa idatha yami kuseva". Umsebenzisi nayo yonke inqubekelaphambili yakhe kuyasuswa kuseva. Uma unqamula ngaphandle kokukhetha lokho, idatha ihlala kuseva. Uma ungasenawo uhlelo lokusebenza noma isifundi se-e, bhalela lecturameter.app@gmail.com ngegama lakho lomsebenzisi lokuvumelanisa bese sisusa umsebenzisi nayo yonke inqubekelaphambili yakhe kuseva ezinsukwini ezingu-30 ubuningi.

9. Izimvume zohlelo lokusebenza

Android:

  • I-inthanethi: ukuseshela izincwadi, ukwenza ikhophi yesipele ku-Drive, ukuthumela impendulo, ukuthola amakhodi, ukuqala ukuzama mahhala, nokuthumela izibalo ezingaziwa negama nezinkombandlela zokuphahlazeka (uma kuvuliwe).
  • Isitoreji sasendaweni nezithombe: ukulondoloza nokufunda amakhophi akho esipele namakhava ezincwadi kudivayisi.
  • Ikhamera: ukuskena ibhakhodi yencwadi (i-ISBN) futhi, uma ufuna, ukuthatha izithombe zomgogodla nemiphetho yekhophi yakho. Lezo zithombe zigcinwa kudivayisi yakho nasemakhophini akho esipele (uma kuvuliwe) kuphela, futhi azithunyelwa kunoma kuphi kwenye indawo.
  • Izaziso: ngesitophuwashi seseshini yokufunda esebenzayo nokukwazisa ngesifinyezo sakho seviki nalapho i-Wrapped yakho isilungile.
  • Ukufinyelela kokungaphazamisi, kuphela uma uvula okungaphazamisi okuzenzakalelayo emodini yokufunda ebhucungayo: ukunikeza ezilungiselelweni zesistimu futhi ungakhipha noma nini. Isitophuwashi sokufunda sisebenza njengesevisi engaphambili enesaziso esiqhubekayo, ukuze isikhathi esidlulile sihlale sinembile ngenkathi uhlelo lokusebenza lusemuva.
  • Iakhawunti ye-Google (OAuth2, ekhethwayo): kuphela uma uvula ikhophi yesipele ye-Drive. Ungakhipha ukufinyelela noma nini kusuka kuakhawunti yakho ye-Google.

iOS:

  • Ikhamera: ukuskena ibhakhodi yencwadi (i-ISBN) futhi, uma ufuna, ukuthatha izithombe zomgogodla nemiphetho yekhophi yakho. Lezo zithombe zigcinwa kudivayisi yakho nasemakhophini akho esipele (uma kuvuliwe) kuphela, futhi azithunyelwa kunoma kuphi kwenye indawo.
  • Izithombe: kuphela esithombeni osikhethela njengekhava yencwadi; uhlelo lokusebenza alufinyeleli kwezinye izithombe zakho.
  • Izaziso: ngesitophuwashi seseshini yokufunda esebenzayo, i-Live Activity nezikhumbuzo zokufunda.
  • I-iCloud (ekhethwayo): kuphela uma uvula ikhophi yesipele ye-iCloud. Ungayivala noma nini kusuka ezilungiselelweni ze-iCloud kudivayisi yakho.
  • Ukufinyelela kweakhawunti ye-Google, kuphela uma uvula ikhophi yesipele ye-Google Drive; ungakhipha noma nini kusuka kuakhawunti yakho ye-Google.

10. Abantu besithathu

Ku-Android: I-Google Play Billing icubungula inkokhelo ye-Pro; uhlelo lokusebenza alukaze lubone idatha yakho yokukhokha. I-Google Drive igcina amakhophi akho esipele akhethwayo kuakhawunti yakho ye-Google, futhi uhlelo lokusebenza lubona kuphela amafayela olidale. Isikena sebhakhodi (Google ML Kit) nesikena sedokhumenti (izinsizakalo ze-Google Play) zicubungula izithombe kudivayisi yakho; i-Google ingase yamukele izilinganiso zokusetshenziswa nokusebenza nezihlonzi zokufaka ngayinye kulezi zingxenye, ngaphansi kwenqubomgomo yobumfihlo ye-Google uqobo. Amakhathalogu ezincwadi omphakathi amukela usesho lwakho ngeseva yethu, njengoba kuchaziwe ngenhla. I-Cloudflare ibamba amaseva e-Lecturameter. Asithengisi idatha yakho noma siyabelane ngayo ekukhangiseni.

Ku-iOS: I-App Store icubungula inkokhelo ye-Pro; uhlelo lokusebenza alukaze lubone idatha yakho yokukhokha. I-Google Drive ingagcina amakhophi akho esipele akhethwayo kuakhawunti yakho ye-Google, futhi uhlelo lokusebenza lubona kuphela amafayela olidale. Amakhathalogu ezincwadi omphakathi amukela usesho lwakho ngeseva yethu, njengoba kuchaziwe ngenhla. I-Cloudflare ibamba amaseva e-Lecturameter. Asithengisi idatha yakho noma siyabelane ngayo ekukhangiseni.

Uma unemibuzo ngobumfihlo, bhalela lecturameter.app@gmail.com.

Uma le nqubomgomo ishintsha, sizobuyekeza leli khasi elifanayo ngosuku olusha ngaphezulu.
