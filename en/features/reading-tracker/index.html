Reading Tracker for Android

Track what you read, when you read and how you read. Private, offline, no account.

Get Lecturameter on Google Play

Your library, your rules

Add a book by searching, scanning the ISBN barcode, or typing it manually. The book gets a cover, an author, a page count and any editions you want to add.

Nothing you add is uploaded. Nothing needs an account. The library lives on your phone.

States and dates

Each book has one of three states. Reading, finished, pending. Move it as you read. Lecturameter records the date automatically so your history stays honest without you having to think about it.

Notes, quotes and rating

Every book has room for private notes and quotes. You can rate it in half stars. You can mark it as reread. You can leave a comment on the whole book or on a single session.

Progress that means something

Progress is a number, not a percentage bar. You know the pages you have left, the average pace you have been reading at, and, if you want, an honest estimate of how long the book will take you at that pace.

History

Every session, every book you finished, every day you read is in the history. Not a leaderboard. Not a social feed. Just the record of what you have been reading, so you can look back at it.
