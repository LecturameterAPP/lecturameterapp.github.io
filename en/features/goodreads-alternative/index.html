What you get

Lecturameter is a reading tracker for Android. It gives you what most people actually want from Goodreads and skips the rest.

Your library is yours. Your reading history is yours. Your notes and quotes are yours. There is no social feed and no algorithm. There is a timer, pages per minute, monthly stats, yearly recap, custom challenges and a bingo card if you feel like it.

Bring your library from anywhere

Lecturameter reads exports from Goodreads, StoryGraph, Bookmory, Fable / ShelfBridge and Book Tracker. Your book list, dates and ratings come across. You keep the shelves you want, drop the ones you do not use. If your export file is a standard CSV, Lecturameter can probably read it.

What Lecturameter does not do

Lecturameter is not a social network. There are no followers, no feed and no book clubs. There is no place to publish reviews to strangers.

If that side of Goodreads matters to you, Lecturameter is not a full replacement. If what you want is a private, precise, offline reading log, it probably is.

Frequently asked questions

Goodreads: goodreads.com > My Books > Import and export > Export Library, you get a CSV. StoryGraph: app.thestorygraph.com > Manage account > Import/Export > Export STG Library, the CSV arrives by email. Bookmory: Settings > Backup > Export. Fable / ShelfBridge: use the ShelfBridge extension. Once you have the CSV or file, import it into Lecturameter from Settings > Import/Export.
Book list, ratings, dates and shelves. Reviews and private notes if the export includes them are stored as private notes on each book.
Goodreads, StoryGraph, Bookmory, Fable / ShelfBridge, Book Tracker (CSV from Settings > Export), and any app that exports a CSV compatible with the Goodreads or StoryGraph format (Babelio, Pagebound, Bookshelf, Delibrista and similar).
